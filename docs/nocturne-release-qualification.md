# Nocturne qualification on 1,000 stories

This protocol extends the [SatinLore release acceptance gates](release-acceptance.md) for a controlled English-language release. Freeze the thresholds, corpus and candidate before testing. The 20-work published diagnostic, earlier original stories and any material used to tune Nocturne belong in development. A live founder result describes the tested prompt and passages. Release qualification also requires the actual reader, deployed service, playback controls and advertised device configurations to pass.

## Assemble and seal the corpus

Choose **1,000 complete, permission-cleared works from at least 400 authors**, with at most four works per author. Sample from a permissioned inventory using a recorded seed and stratified random draws. Record the available and selected counts, source limitations and reasons for replacement before scoring. Count one work per underlying story, even when the same work has multiple editions, chapters or mirrors. Group authors, co-authors, series, adaptations, translations and near duplicates together. Remove any group that overlaps development, including the new 20-work diagnostic. Check exact digests, normalized text similarity and shared story metadata before the seal.

| Highest depiction level in the work | 500–2,000 words | Over 2,000–8,000 | Over 8,000–20,000 | Over 20,000 within import limits | Total |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Neutral or nonromantic hard negative | 50 | 40 | 25 | 10 | 125 |
| Romance or sensual tension without depicted sexual activity | 80 | 90 | 55 | 25 | 250 |
| Implied or non-graphic adult intimacy | 95 | 110 | 65 | 30 | 300 |
| Graphic adult material with brief or intermittent detail | 55 | 60 | 40 | 20 | 175 |
| Graphic adult material with sustained detail | 45 | 50 | 35 | 20 | 150 |
| **Total** | **325** | **350** | **220** | **105** | **1,000** |

Set source quotas of 400 works from permissioned online fiction collections, 250 from directly participating independent authors, 200 from permissioned reader-submitted material and 150 from rights-verified published archives. Assign each work to one acquisition channel. Source from at least six independent collections or communities and cap any one source family at 25%. Include at least 200 newly published or privately licensed works unavailable to the development team during tuning. Document possible model pretraining overlap where it cannot be checked. Report the balanced test mix and, when a consented aggregate usage distribution is available, a separately weighted result for that distribution. Keep the release thresholds on the fixed test mix.

Tag overlapping style and difficulty slices, including contemporary, historical, speculative, suspense or mixed genre, dialogue-led, description-led, subtle, sustained, long quiet opening and abrupt changes. Include varied adult relationship dynamics, pairings and perspectives. Balance current scenes, memories, reports, imagination, ambiguous passages, changes of narrative frame, clear withdrawals and hard scene cuts. Include stories where paragraphs mix a cue and a stop. An advertised slice needs at least 30 works from 20 author groups and 100 relevant reference anchors for each gated score. If the inventory cannot support that, record the scope gap before release. Test one-section stories, unsupported languages, malformed imports and inputs at and beyond the import limits separately as controlled regressions.

The private manifest records source edition and digest, rights evidence and approved uses, adult eligibility review, language, grouping, import route, length and depiction level. Verify each work and any provider excerpts against the actual rights and access terms. Get separate permission for local evaluation, hosted excerpt processing and redistribution where those uses apply. Exclude uncertain age or ineligible material from hosted requests and use neutral fixtures to exercise protective behavior. Public Git receives the rubric, runner and aggregate evidence. Private text, permission records, raw predictions with text, credentials and participant records stay in access-controlled storage. Keep a withdrawal and deletion procedure and replace withdrawn evaluation material before the freeze. Invalidate any affected result after the freeze rather than silently swapping sources.

## Build the reference independently

Train adult reviewers on a separate pilot set. Obtain explicit opt-in for the depiction levels each reviewer is willing to read. Provide private skip and withdrawal controls, without requiring a reason. Route skipped items to another consenting reviewer. Record incomplete annotation and resolve gaps before scoring.

Two independent reviewers read each complete work in the imported order and label every imported paragraph before seeing predictions or each other's labels. Retain both first-pass records. A third reviewer resolves disagreements using the text and rubric, with the model output concealed. Check pre-adjudication Cohen’s κ of at least 0.70 for cue eligibility and event kind. Review boundary disagreement and agreement by slice. If the pilot falls short, revise the rubric and repeat on fresh pilot material. Any test material already viewed during tuning becomes development evidence.

| Label | What the reference captures |
| --- | --- |
| Cue eligibility and text band | Supported cue, clear negative, uncertain or excluded. Keep the existing 0–4 local score and the 0–3 exact scene emphasis on their own scales, separate from the reader’s permitted intensity. Mark hard quiet and explicit stops. |
| Category and depiction | Neutral, romance, flirting, tension, intimacy, explicit, aftermath or abstain. Mark the 0–4 depiction level separately and record evidence offsets. A detailed passage can still be a report, memory, stop or quiet. |
| Event kind | Current, imagined, reported, historical, uncertain or neutral. Use narrative evidence to resolve apparent tense changes. |
| Phase | Quiet, build, sustain, peak or recover. Record episodes and valid continuation, with separate spans for mixed paragraphs. |
| Transition and its evidence | Mark the earliest sentence that changes or ends the active state. Record before and after anchors, the transition type and whether an explicit stop or hard quiet applies. Distinguish enacted changes from a dated entry, incidental activity or a discussion of an event. |
| Supported pattern evidence | Record only clearly grounded movement or rhythm cues. Map capability and calibration in the separate playback run. |

Before sealing, require at least 8,000 clear positive cue anchors, 8,000 neutral or ambiguous anchors, 2,000 phase transitions, 300 decisive stop or hard-quiet transitions and 200 eligible Loom misses across at least 50 author groups. Keep all eligible anchors in the scored denominator. Freeze source and rubric digests, reviewer decisions, pairing and sampling plans, expected metrics, mandatory slices, statistical rules, candidate and baseline commit SHAs, provider and prompt versions, runtime settings and allowed retries. Seal the label files and report only anonymous aggregate identifiers to implementers until the score is final.

## Score the same traversal at each layer

Run baseline and candidate on identical text and cursor traces. Cover paste, TXT, supported link import and paused restore where the work can be used on that route. Traverse the whole work in a deterministic replay. Replay the first 3,600 words and a fixed middle start at 120, 240 and 420 words per minute. Use the remaining text when shorter. Run matched 10- and 15-minute elapsed-time traces at those speeds to capture the effect of how much text the reader reaches. Include a longer trace for the optional edging setting and compare build, ease and repeat behavior without using a physiological outcome as a score. Add fixed long dwell, reverse scroll, fast seeks, reflow, hide and return cases. Keep eligible words, attempted requests, usable responses, effective cues, simulator targets and command dispatch as separate records.

| Check | Pass condition for the frozen candidate |
| --- | --- |
| Effective contextual Nocturne | At least **92% cue precision** and **90% cue recall** on the same traversed reference text. Neutral false-cue rate at most 5%. Recover at least half of eligible Loom misses on covered anchors and report recovery across all eligible Loom misses. |
| Coverage and abstention | In each supported slice, at least 80% of reference-eligible words have usable annotations by paragraph exit at 240 words per minute and at least 70% at 120 and 420. Apply this to both prefix and middle starts. Show whole-story coverage separately. Count false exclusions, skipped windows, refusals, timeouts, rate limits, invalid responses, cancellation and quota exhaustion by reason. An eligible word without an on-time usable annotation remains in the coverage denominator. |
| Label quality | Event-kind macro-F1 at least 0.85 and phase macro-F1 at least 0.80 on covered adjudicated anchors. Count abstention as an error on clear anchors. Include confusion matrices and support counts for category, frame and phase, and report first-person, past-tense and mixed-frame errors by slice. |
| Phase transitions and stops | At least 85% precision and recall with maximum one-to-one matching within one imported paragraph. Report exact sentence anchors separately, alongside performance by boundary type. A wrong boundary kind does not match. Preserve every explicit stop and hard quiet in the playback checks. |
| Story-level reliability | At least 90% of works with five or more clear positive anchors have at least 80% cue recall and 85% cue precision. For remaining works, report the positive denominator and require zero unsupported cues on each wholly clear-negative story. Each supported content-level and length slice requires at least 85% recall and 90% precision, alongside the global gate. |
| Loom and fallback | Retain the existing Loom gates. Score all traversed anchors using the actual local fallback when contextual analysis cannot be used. Keep separate no-provider, provider-only and effective-reader counts. A missing, invalid or abstained contextual result is a provider miss on a clear positive, even if Loom correctly recovers it. |
| Live provider and spend | On the signed-in candidate, at least 95% of ordinary eligible requests return usable labels in eight seconds. Keep failures in the denominator and preserve ordinary retry and quota behavior. For a 3,600-word comparison, p95 attempted use is at most 25,000 input and 12,000 output tokens per session and repeated submitted source is at most 1.25 times unique submitted bytes on the sequential trace. Reconcile billed retries and partial requests. Unknown usage leaves the spend gate open. |
| Privacy and cancellation | Zero unconsented, out-of-bounds or reference-protected excerpt submissions, text leakage, persisted contextual consent or application after cancellation in scripted tests. Verify that the local selection screen excludes protected spans before transport and count lost eligible text separately. Exercise wrong origin, unsupported data, omitted and duplicate IDs, provider refusal, timeout, rate limit, failed authentication, quota exhaustion, seek, hide and story switch. Show truthful coverage and fallback. Check the cue-only path separately against its text-free contract. |

Calculate micro scores with full reference denominators and macro scores across stories. Report per-story counts and slice counts alongside pooled values. Use 20,000 fixed-seed bootstrap replicates over author and linked-series groups to compute 95% intervals for pooled scores and paired changes from the baseline. Resample whole groups so repeated paragraphs and replayed traces contribute correlated observations. Preserve the sampling channel in the resampling plan and account for any population weights explicitly. Require the lower 95% bound to meet the global cue precision, cue recall and transition thresholds. Use simultaneous 95% lower bounds across predeclared supported slices for the slice floors, with a Bonferroni adjustment within each metric family. Report the upper bound for false-cue rates. An interval crossing a required limit leaves the gate open. Zero observed violations is a gate on the executed cases, with the tested counts recorded.

## Verify playback and the reading experience

Replay the same annotated stories and cursor traces through the actual simulator with default, tight and zero calibration. Separate text recognition, renderer contrast, transport and control behavior. Follow the existing [output and user control gates](release-acceptance.md#output-and-user-control-gates). Require at least 90% contrast in isolated renderer episodes with headroom and three seconds of dwell, then at least 75% through the full Nocturne pipeline and 65% through Loom. Require p95 stable-cursor to expected dispatch of at most two seconds and positive-target zero gaps at most 2% of sequential positive-target time. Count intentional zeros separately. Test Start, Stop, Pause, manual zero, lowered ceiling, ended action, hide, inactivity, disconnect, logout, restore and stale responses with zero violations. Record import and control evidence on the actual supported browser and phone combinations.

Qualify each advertised physical model, firmware, Remote version, browser and action on the desk first. Observe three successful repeats per Stop, finite expiry and interruption case. Require zero wrong-device or wrong-action commands, two-second finite nonzero commands, healthy-connection Stop within one second and transport-loss expiry within the last accepted command lifetime plus the predeclared tolerance of at most 250 ms. Gate configurations independently. [Hardware test record](hardware-test-record.md)

For the larger reading pilot, enroll **100 consenting adult readers** with defined eligibility and collect at least **80 completed paired sessions**. Preserve the existing first-ten completion requirement. Counterbalance condition order with a recorded random assignment, use comparable story portions and the same reader calibration, and keep variant identity concealed where practical. Use the current qualified experience as the comparator. Participants choose acceptable content levels, can pause or withdraw, and can skip sensitive questions. Separate participation from research recording, any hardware use and any optional intimate feedback. Keep rewards independent of results and public reviews. Record perceived pacing, quiet-scene engagement and correction burden for the first 10–15 minutes and for the separately selected extended mode.

At least 80% of enrolled readers must complete import, setup, read, adjustment and Stop unaided. Among completed pairs, at least 75% must rate both candidate timing fit and control comfort at least 4/5, and median deliberate corrections must be at most one per ten minutes. For a reading-preference claim, require at least 60% of all completed pairs to prefer the candidate, with ties and skips counted separately within that denominator and a lower 95% participant-level confidence bound above 50%. Report early exits, failed attempts, distraction, background-versus-pattern preference and optional enjoyment feedback separately. Report optional answers with their actual response count and keep interpretation to self-reported experience. Resolve and retest any serious control or privacy issue before launch.

## Run and decide

1. Run `python3 scripts/check.py`, `npm run build:worker`, `npm run test:browser`, `node scripts/nocturne-browser-check.mjs` and the [QA checklist](qa.md). Use `node scripts/evaluate-nocturne-corpus.mjs /path/to/private/manifest.json --validate-only` to check compatible private manifest shards. The default reference replay tests the evaluator against reference data. Live-provider modes and the [founder study](../deploy/cloudflare/README.md#run-the-founder-story-study) are bounded diagnostics with their own request limits.
2. Build the full 1,000-work runner with manifest-shard verification, complete denominators, the real reader traversal, release interval calculations and a resumable request ledger before starting the scored run. Predeclare the call and cost plan. Use separate authenticated staging resources for large-scale evaluation while preserving product limits in the actual reader replays. Keep story text out of logs, analytics, URLs, device traffic and error reports. Record the exact service and storage settings, consent checks, provider behavior, per-request outcomes and rollback rehearsal.
3. Have a reviewer independent of tuning sign the frozen decision record. Include build SHA, model and prompt, source and rubric versions, coverage, counts, intervals, confidence-bound rules, token usage, simulator and hardware evidence, participant results and pass/fail/open status. Keep a one-row decision for every mandatory slice and advertised configuration.

Block launch if any mandatory threshold misses, a required slice lacks evidence, a bound crosses its threshold, a serious control or privacy incident is open, provider spend cannot be reconciled, or the proposed claim exceeds the tested scope. If scoring leads to a code, prompt, threshold or rubric change, move that run to development and use a fresh sealed release set. A narrowed scope needs its own predeclared evaluation and matching release copy.
