YOUR READING SPACE

A little help, when you need it.

Read immediately, make adjustments when you want, and keep control throughout. Try a model-specific simulator, or connect one toy through Lovense Remote. Physical compatibility depends on the model, the movements your toy reports and your connection.

Start reading

  1. Write or paste into the editor, then choose Read story. Choose Add a story for a fresh page.
  2. Your story opens immediately. Suggested intensities are prepared on this device; review is optional.
  3. Choose Connect simulator. When ready, choose Start and confirm the maximum.
  4. Use − / + to lower or raise the current section one level at a time. Pause and Stop both ask the toy to stop; another Start always needs confirmation.

First time? Choose Toy setup to pair and check a toy, or rehearse with the simulator. The side preview follows simulated movement as you scroll; the practice story lets you try the level buttons, maximum and Stop before your own story.

Stop keeps your story open and the simulator connected. The message beside Start explains anything needed before it can be used again.

Connect a toy, or rehearse without one

  1. Choose Toy setup in the header. Find your model in the list.
  2. Choose Try a simulator for a hardware-free rehearsal. For a physical toy, choose Connect my toy, pair it in Lovense Remote on your phone, then follow the private QR instructions on a second screen.
  3. Select the discovered device yourself. Model choice alone does not connect or start it. Use the movement cards to choose what to use and adjust your levels. On a physical toy, run the short test for each selected movement, beginning at level 1.
  4. Confirm that each physical movement started and stopped. Apply your settings, then try the optional test read. Practice the level buttons, maximum, scrolling, Pause, Stop and restart before opening your own story.

The simulators have distinct toy previews and movement channels. Rehearsal tools let you try a disconnected or failed connection, then reconnect without restarting playback. No Lovense account or developer credential is needed in simulation.

Physical commands target your selected toy and expire after two seconds. Each short test starts in the gentle range. Stop is a request; the graphic cannot measure your toy. Keep its physical button and Lovense Remote available. If the connection drops, use those controls and reconnect explicitly. Raising the physical maximum pauses for a fresh Start; there is no extra popup.

Extra movements appear when both the reader and your connected toy support them. If live connection is unavailable, you can still try a simulator. Your local story text is never sent to Lovense.

Bring a story with you

Under Add a story, choose Use a link, paste an AO3 work or chapter URL and choose Check link. Public works can import when AO3 makes them available. A work link requests the whole work; a chapter link imports that chapter. If the page needs sign-in or cannot be fetched, use AO3’s Download → HTML and upload the file.

Links to Literotica and other supported source sites offer a link to the original page. For analysis here, use your own text or material you have permission to analyse. Choose Upload a file for a TXT file or an AO3 HTML download.

A deliberate AO3 link import sends that public link to SatinLore to fetch the story. It does not forward your source-site login or save the story on the server. Pasted text and file uploads stay on your device unless you separately choose account saving or contextual Nocturne.

If playback is unavailable

Start is unavailable
Read the message beside Start. You may need to open a story, wait for suggestions, connect the simulator or check your sign-in.
Another tab is using the simulator
Stop playback in the other tab, then return here and choose Start. A single browser session plays at a time.
Your sign-in cannot be checked
Choose Check sign-in again. If a new login is needed, save any session-only story before reloading. Your readable text stays on the page while playback is paused.
Disconnected or interrupted
Reconnect when ready, then confirm Start. Returning to a tab or changing a setting never starts a movement automatically.
A save or backup fails
Keep this tab open. Your in-memory story is still readable. Retry saving or download a backup before closing it.

Your library and backups

Search by title, sort your library and switch stories from the library or open-story tabs. Choose New Shelf in the Library, then Move current story here, or choose a shelf under Story settings. Removing a shelf moves its stories to Unfiled. To revise a story, choose Edit text as a copy in Advanced. The edited copy opens for this visit and you can save it separately. Switching stories pauses playback.

Session only is the default. Save on this browser keeps your text, title, corrections and reading position in this browser profile. Restored stories are paused; temporary level adjustments and playback permissions are never restored.

Open the top-right Settings gear for Import & backup. Restored copies start in Unfiled; folder names are kept separately on this browser.

A browser library backup includes stories saved here and open session-only stories. Back up account stories individually. Keep your downloads private because they contain story text. Restoring a backup adds session-only copies and labels duplicate titles. Choose Save on this browser to keep them here.

Browser saves stay on this device. Other people using the same browser profile may see them. Clearing browser data can remove them. If you have a public reader account, choose Save to my account to open a story on your other devices.

Make the workspace yours

Close either sidebar for more reading room, or use Focus to close both. The sliders icon opens Reading controls for the story model, text size, spacing, width and optional section corrections. Use the top-right Dark mode switch to change between Satin Editorial and Midnight Ink. The separate Settings gear also offers the device appearance setting, backups, account information and support. Close it with Close or the top-right ×. Layout changes preserve your section and pause playback.

Use the maximum slider directly during simulation; it does not open a popup or restart playback. Start still asks you to confirm the current maximum. Strength changes stay within that maximum. Fresh preferences use Continuous background and Follow scene rhythm, with Steady hold for Nocturne peaks. Existing saved choices stay as you set them. You can change these in Settings. Stop, a maximum of zero and a manual zero still stop movement.

Both models follow the story’s pacing. Nocturne offers additional patterns for scene peaks. Choose either model on an analysed story. The short calibration stories use fixed levels. Sign in to save presets to your account. Choose a model before adding a story or change it while reading. Midnight Ink is the default for a fresh reader. You can switch to Satin Editorial or follow your device’s appearance.

Corrections are optional. Review initially shows only the current and already-read sections. Upcoming sections stay hidden until you choose to reveal them. A correction of zero always means zero; analysing again keeps your corrections.

Use your own maximum and adjust a section if a suggestion feels wrong.

Privacy and your app data

Loom analyses your story on this device. Optional contextual Nocturne is explained below. Drafts stay in the open tab until you choose where to save them. Browser-saved stories are available in this browser profile. Toy commands never include your story.

Save to my account keeps a selected story, its details, corrections and progress for other devices. Account changes show Saving until the service confirms them. This tab can remember the latest section of an account story if you reload before its progress finishes saving.

Public accounts use an email verification code at registration, then your email and password to sign in. SatinLore stores a protected password hash. Amazon SES delivers verification and reset codes. Cloudflare hosts the reader and account library. The sign-in cookie lasts up to 12 hours, or up to 30 days when you choose Keep me signed in. Reading preferences and toy presets can sync through your account.

Deleting your account removes your password sign-in, profile, account stories and progress, reading preferences and toy presets, and signs you out on all devices. Stories saved in this browser stay here. Manage submitted feedback separately on the Feedback & data page. Cloudflare recovery history may temporarily retain earlier account copies.

In this private preview, Cloudflare Access handles sign-in and toy presets can sync through your preview account. Stories saved on this browser stay in this browser. Removing the preview profile and presets is separate from signing out, browser-story deletion, submitted feedback and any separately consented beta recording.

Feedback is sent when you submit it. Please leave out story excerpts and other private details. Your Feedback & data page lets you view, export and delete your submitted app feedback.

Submitted feedback expires after 30 days. Message-free abuse-prevention counters expire within 24 hours and remain separate from feedback deletion. Feedback deletion removes the live app copy. Cloudflare recovery history may retain previous copies for up to another 30 days.

Optional contextual Nocturne

The cue-only Nocturne action sends fixed categories without story words. If you separately enable contextual analysis for the current story, nearby excerpts pass through SatinLore to OpenAI as you read. Each request includes at most eight paragraphs and 12,000 bytes of text. Coverage is limited by the displayed session allowance and beta quotas. Titles, source links and device settings are excluded.

Contextual annotations and your choice stay in this tab’s memory and are cleared when you disable the mode or leave its reading session. SatinLore does not save excerpts in the analysis service or send them to Lovense. Refused, uncertain and unavailable results use local analysis. This does not verify age or consent or predict your response.

OpenAI response storage is disabled for these calls. Separate provider abuse-monitoring rules can retain content for up to 30 days under standard controls. Read OpenAI’s data controls before enabling. Choosing account saving and choosing contextual analysis are separate actions.
