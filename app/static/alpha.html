Device movements and playback

Choose a movement and set a low starting level. Press Start when you’re ready to try it.

What to try
  1. Find a comfortable level. Try one movement at a time. Adjust Level and set a Personal maximum for each.
  2. Try a change of pace. Compare a steady rhythm with a varying one. Try a low Continuous minimum, then try zero to compare.
  3. Test Ease off. Pick a lower level, then press Ease off to see how it feels.
  4. Try your checked movements together at different levels.

You can stop at any time. When you’re done testing, choose Finish & stop.

Hold keeps the current levels steady. Resume levels brings back the levels and rhythms you chose.
